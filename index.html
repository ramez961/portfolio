FORM Athletic Club

A gym that welcomes every starting point.

Concept & brandHTML / CSS / JSAccessible, framework-free
Visit the website ↗Read the case study ↗View source ↗
The website’s purpose
Introduce a fictional athletic club to everyday athletes and guide visitors from a first look to a starting program and a membership.
The design
A deep navy and cool off-white base with a bright mint accent for every action and electric blue for emphasis, and tight geometric headlines softened by a single italic serif word.
The experience
A program planner, class timetable, BMI calculator, membership pricing toggle, demo shop and six coach profiles, all running in the browser with nothing sent anywhere.
Knowledge brought together
Brand direction, UX writing, responsive layout, accessibility, and framework-free front-end implementation.

Brennar

Outdoor essentials, with a clear path from discovery to the bag.

Storefront conceptCatalog dashboardHTML / CSS / JS
View source ↗
The website’s purpose
Introduce a fictional outdoor gear brand and help visitors explore a sample product catalog, select options, and preview a shopping bag.
The design
Olive and charcoal, condensed display typography, cinematic mountain imagery, and product photographs presented in full with clear labels below.
The experience
Search, category filters, saved gear, product details, and a persistent bag with demo checkout. A browser-local studio manages products, custom categories, and image collections; no real orders or payments are submitted.
The performance
Photo assets reduced from 25.3 MB to 584 KB — 97.7% smaller — with responsive WebP images, deferred scripts, lazy loading, and compression in the preview server.
